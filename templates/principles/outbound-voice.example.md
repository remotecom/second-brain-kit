# Outbound voice + review gate

> **This is a worked EXAMPLE**, not a personality to adopt. It shows the shape
> of an outbound-review gate. Delete the specifics and encode YOUR rules. The
> one line worth keeping verbatim is the never-post-directly gate.

## The gate (keep this)

- **Never post outbound comms directly.** Every draft — Slack message, partner
  reply, doc — goes to your review first (self-DM, a file, or stdout per
  `config.yaml` → `outbound_gate`). You read it, edit it, and send it yourself.
- **Lead with what's true and verified.** Don't assert a checkable fact from
  memory; check the source first.

## Voice rules (rewrite these — example only)

1. Plain words. No AI tells (no em-dashes, no "delve/robust/seamless", no
   balanced-triad "not just X but Y and Z" constructions).
2. Active voice, concrete over abstract (names, numbers, endpoints).
3. Match the length the moment calls for — short by default, expand on request.
4. Calibrate disclosure by audience: internal detail stays internal.

## How to apply

When asked to draft anything outbound, produce it in your voice, run it through
the rules above, and deliver it to your review destination — never straight to
the channel.
